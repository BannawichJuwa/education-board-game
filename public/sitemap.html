/loginLogin / Register (and Account Profile)Page
Everyone
  • Log In tab: username and password with a show / hide button
  • Register tab: full name, username, password and confirm password (username at least 3 characters, password at least 4)
  • After login the Teacher Dashboard opens; sessions are then saved in the database
  • When logged in this page shows the Account Profile: name, member since, last login
  • Log Out with confirmation (back to Guest Mode)
  • Delete Account: asks for the password and deletes the account and all its data

/sessionsSaved SessionsPage
Everyone (guest sessions are deleted after 24 hours)
  • Guest Mode banner: data is deleted after 24 hours, time left, Log In link
  • Search by session title or subject
  • Filter tabs: All, In Progress, Completed
  • Session card: Continue Playing (in progress) opens /game/{sessionId}
  • Session card: View Scoreboard (completed) opens /scoreboard/{sessionId}
  • Session card: Restart Game and Delete, each with a confirmation window (UI-12)
